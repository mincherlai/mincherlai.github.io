# mincherlai.github.io

Personal, non-commercial academic homepage of Dr. Min-Cherl Jung
(GitHub Pages, served from `main`).

## Publishing workflow
- Before publishing, describe the proposed change to the owner in chat
  (in Korean; include a screenshot when the change is visual).
- Once the owner approves, publish it to the live site immediately:
  push, open the PR, mark it ready, and merge it into `main` yourself.
  Do not ask the owner to visit or click anything on GitHub.
