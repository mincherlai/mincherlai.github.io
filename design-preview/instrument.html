About

The laboratory quantifies defect species, states and densities in halide perovskite thin films — and correlates them with quantitative terahertz absorption measured on the same films. Surfaces and interfaces are read with synchrotron photoelectron spectroscopy; the lattice is read with THz-TDS. Led by Min-Cherl Jung, Associate Professor (Research), Global Institute of Future Technology, Shanghai Jiao Tong University.
