01About

The laboratory studies how atomic-scale defects and lattice dynamics decide what a halide perovskite can actually do. Defect species, states and densities are quantified, and correlated with terahertz absorption measured on the same films.

It is led by Min-Cherl Jung, Associate Professor (Research) at the Global Institute of Future Technology, Shanghai Jiao Tong University.
